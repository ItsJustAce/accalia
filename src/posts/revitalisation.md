it has been a moment or two since i have been present on the internet. i think it has caught up to me in some senses, because i miss my time on here. i have been very fortunate to have had some time away from study and work. i would like to say i spent it all relaxing, but this has not been the case entirely. some personal shit has gone down and continues to keep going down but thats okay. i feel at a space where creation is the way forward.
## what am i doing here?
a decision that i decided, and not lightly, to take was to take a gap year for studies. i realised a few things simultaneously this year:

1. i, to the surprise of no one, have uncovered an ADD diagnosis. i think about all the times this totally shouldve made sense. i just never sort of considered it to be... possible for me? like it could never happen. but here we are i guess. go get that checked if you suspect it. i have grieved my past self and onto forgiving me. this is the biggest realisation
2. making food consistently and self-care are necessary habits to have and currently i do not have them. i do not function at a basic level consistently. a hard thing to achieve with ADD apparently, yet directly affects my daily life obviously.
3. i hate my course. i am studying a bachelors in comp sci, and i hate it. my uni is shit and my course is shit and the people on it are shit. granted, this could be my perception due to my now very apparent neurodivergencies, but i digress. i cannot fathom staying around right now. i wanted an environment of people who care about the course and the subject in the way i do, but it is non-existent and not fostered. the most fun i had was an in person game jam which was not even hosted by my course.

this combination of events leads into the biggest eventuality: medication. i am stuck as a person in the uk and that also means a shit level of healthcare. it took nearly a year to receive my diagnosis and i have now been waiting over ~~7~~ 10 months for medication to even begin to be on the table for me while i wait to be assigned a psychiatrist. and all i can do is wait and try other things. i have a couple things that i could try but they involve finding communities which as it currently stands i struggle to engage with consistently. i need some proper guidance.

so, the gap year makes sense. i can spend time thinking about whats next in my life and decide if the course is right for me, whilst waiting for the medication to come into my life and hopefully make a nice change. or atleast put me on the path towards it. im moving back home for the foreseeable future. i am aware many dont have this opportunity, so i am eternally grateful to have a home to return to with open arms. money remains an issue, i had a job briefly, before they fired me for calling in sick too many times so thats cool. its hard not to feel like an inconvenience to the world at large sometimes, but i stick my finger to it and keep going anyway. i was listening to *remember i love you - kimya dawson* and i think "the challenge" resonates with me deeply for this reason. i refuse to take this shit lying down (comically i am lying horizontally right now - chronic fatigue a bitch, but still my figurative point stands).

i need some time to reorient and build some better structures and support in my tiny existence to continue to stand tall like the swirling, spinning, out-of-balance tower of Pisa that i am. i think everyone feels this way for a long amount of time, and even if they dont i dont care. i am the only comparison to make as i am the only me.
## okay idc, whats the plan creative wise?
so this is where i am unsure. standing at the brow of a hill looking out at all the land the light touches showing me what could be mine. its just unlike light i cant be in every place at once. i have to pick my projects like battles (if only battles you could give up before youve even started like my projects, then maybe battles wouldnt kill so many people). i want a solid plan or structure to develop for my projects. anyways, a couple of loose ideas:
- remap website for 11ty! I want to make a static site generator setup for my website so I don't have to keep redoing everything. like nav bars or layouts etc. I've never used eleventy, so this'll be fun [future edit - this is now done!]
- work on a separate project to this site - a couple ideas hover for this, including a poetry social media site. idk how useful that would be, though.
- music music muisc - i fucking love making music, so itd be important to me to continue making more
my biggest indentifiable issue is that when i forget about projects, i find myself becoming confused when returning. looking at my website right now after i havent really written an update in a couple months is feeling like a monumental task. to circumvent this time of getting the ball rolling again would be enjoyable. or atleast some way of making it easier.
- perhaps a game of some kind, i would love to finish one one of these days. <a href="https://retraace.bandcamp.com/album/tea-time-ep"> soundtrack for my friends gamejam is out that i composed though!</a>

I describe it to people as if you are rolling a giant boulder, its hard to get started but once its going consistently it has momentum and its easier to continually push. but when you stop even for a second, getting it rolling again is a big snoozefest. so, like, thats an issue. id like to make the ball rolling again a lot easier, its like i forget how to roll the ball..? idk, the metaphor falls apart now i guess.

whilst a lot of this post feels very negative on reflection, i remain wilfully optimistic for the future of whatever is about to happen. i have a loving partner and i am full of gratitude for what i am lucky to already have.
